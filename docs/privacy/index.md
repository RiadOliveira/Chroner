# Chroner Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Chroner is a to-do app for Android whose main feature is persistent reminders: notifications that stay on your screen until you actively mark the task as complete. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

**In short: Chroner does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.**

## 1. Who we are

Chroner is developed by an independent developer based in Brazil ("we", "us" or "our"). You can contact us at any time:

- Email: dev.riadoliveira@gmail.com

## 2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following information **locally, in the app's private storage on your device**:

- Task name
- Due date and reminder time (both optional)
- Recurrence (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or none)
- Task color
- The internal identifier of the notification scheduled for each task
- Your language preference (Portuguese or English)

The app also reads your device's language setting to choose its default language. This happens locally and the information is not sent anywhere.

This information is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it. Like any Android app, it is isolated from other apps by the operating system.

## 3. Information we do not collect

Chroner does **not**:

- require an account, login or registration;
- collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or any other personal identifiers;
- use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking services;
- use advertising IDs, cookies or similar tracking technologies;
- access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files;
- send your tasks or any other data to servers.

## 4. Permissions and features

Chroner uses Android features that are needed for reminders to work. They operate entirely on your device:

- **Notifications:** to display your reminders, including a button to complete the task directly from the notification. Reminders are persistent: if you swipe one away, the app displays it again until you complete or delete the task.
- **Exact alarms:** to deliver reminders at the exact date and time you chose.
- **Foreground service:** while there is at least one active reminder, the app keeps a low-priority notification running so that Android does not stop the app from restoring dismissed reminders, even after it has been closed for a long time. This notification is removed as soon as there are no active reminders. It does not collect any information.
- **Restoring reminders after a restart:** so that scheduled reminders are not lost when the device is turned off and on again.
- **Do Not Disturb:** the app's notification channel is configured to be allowed to appear in Do Not Disturb mode. You can change this at any time in your device's notification settings.

You can revoke any of these permissions in your device's system settings. Note that, without the notification permission, the app cannot fulfill its main purpose.

## 5. Notification content and lock screen

Reminders display the task name. Depending on your device's lock-screen settings, this content may be visible to other people who can see your screen. You can control this in Android's notification settings.

## 6. Sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we do not collect any. Chroner does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Two points are outside our control and are governed by their own policies:

- **Google Play and Android:** downloading and updating the app through Google Play is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Device backups:** if you have Android backup enabled on your device, Google may back up app data to your Google account according to your own settings. This is managed by you and Google, not by us.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Your tasks remain on your device until you delete them or complete them. Completing a one-time task deletes it; completing a recurring task reschedules it for the next occurrence.

To delete all app data, uninstall Chroner or clear its storage in your device's system settings. Because we hold no copies of your data, we are unable to retrieve or delete it on your behalf.

## 8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage and is not transmitted over the internet. Even so, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and keeping the operating system up to date.

## 9. Your rights

Since we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is no data on our side to access, correct, port or delete: you have full control over it on your own device. This policy is designed to be consistent with applicable data protection laws, such as Brazil's LGPD and the EU's GDPR. If you have any question or request related to privacy, contact us at the email above.

## 10. Children's privacy

Chroner is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
